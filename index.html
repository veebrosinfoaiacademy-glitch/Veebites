The problem

Running a restaurant shouldn't mean running five different systems.

Billing in one app. Kitchen tickets on paper. Inventory in a spreadsheet. Payroll, accounting, and reporting somewhere else again. Every handoff between them is a manual step — a re-typed order, an exported file, a number that doesn't quite match.

FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to our team.

Does VeeBites work without internet?

VeeBites is designed with offline-first front-of-house workflows. If the network drops, essential ordering can continue locally, and queued changes synchronize when connectivity returns. Capabilities that rely on connected services — such as external integrations, cross-location reporting, and AI-assisted analysis — resume once you're back online.

Can VeeBites manage tables?

Yes — floors, table layouts, occupancy, covers, and table-linked orders.

Does VeeBites include a kitchen display?

Yes — live tickets, station workflows, preparation status, dish counts, and prep-time monitoring.

Can VeeBites print kitchen tickets?

Yes — through supported local printer infrastructure. VeeBites can print KOTs, bills, receipts, delivery slips, refunds, and summaries.

Does VeeBites include inventory?

Yes — stock, suppliers, purchases, returns, waste, adjustments, transfers, recipes, production, and reconciliation.

Does VeeBites include accounting?

Yes — chart of accounts, journals, ledger, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, and statements.

Can VeeBites manage employees and payroll?

Yes. VeeBites covers employee profiles, departments and positions, attendance, scheduling, leave and holidays, pay profiles and rules, payroll periods, and payroll runs.

Can VeeBites integrate with other software?

Yes — through supported integrations and synchronization workflows, including accounting, payment gateways, delivery aggregators, fiscal invoice services, inventory systems, and logging and monitoring.

Does VeeBites support multiple locations?

Yes, through its synchronization architecture and optional sync service. Each location keeps its local workflows, and cross-location reporting is available through the sync service.
